U.S. Patent No. 12,417,662

CONDUITS: a password you walk.

CONDUITS is an electronic door lock that reads footstep patterns—rhythm, pressure, and sequence—through a pressure-sensitive pad, using that pattern as the password to lock or unlock a door.

The idea started as a Broadcom MASTERS science fair project and won the Lemelson Award for Invention. With support from the Lemelson Foundation and guidance from mentor Bob Zeidman, it grew into a fully documented, issued U.S. patent.

Now, after four years of dedication, this patent was granted in September 2025.
